### 👨🏻‍💻 About Me

I am a **Software Engineer** graduated from the Universidad de las Fuerzas Armadas ESPE 🎓. Currently, I am a member of the **Core Team at Construex**, where I lead the development of **Construpedia**—an AI-driven ecosystem designed to be the Operating System of the Construction industry.

I specialize in building scalable web platforms and robust backend architectures. I'm passionate about technical rigor, clean code, and solving complex business challenges through automation and AI.

- 🚀 **Currently Leading:** The architecture and deployment of [Construpedia](https://construpedia.ai).
- 🛠️ **Expertise:** Full-Stack development with **Next.js, NestJS, and Django**.
- ☁️ **Cloud & DevOps:** Managing production environments on **AWS** using Docker and NGINX.
- 🏃‍♂️ **Life Balance:** Dedicated to **Calisthenics and Running**, which keep my focus sharp and my discipline high.
